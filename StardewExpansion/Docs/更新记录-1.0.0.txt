老乡来了 v1.0 正式版
内部版本：1.0.0

本次收尾
1. 模组由“水培与酿酒扩展”更名为“老乡来了”，英文名 A Fellow Townsman Arrives。
2. 上古果酒从深紫色改为浅蓝色系，保留深青色瓶颈、果实标签和细长酒瓶造型，外部仍占1×4格。
3. 延续22格酒瓶原料区、1/4/9格作物体型、巨型保底及成酒液体转移保护。

更新与兼容
沿用 pelican_cultivation 模组ID及 Mods/PelicanCultivation 目录，直接替换旧版；不要同时安装两份。
重启游戏再加载存档，已有上古果酒会使用新贴图，评分、陈酿价值、基因、种植进度和保底记录继续保留。
运行目录只保留加载必需文件；说明、工坊文案、源码、美术原图与提示词位于运行目录之外。
工坊中英文介绍已随包提供；本次没有向Steam上传或发布。

验证
编译、13,064项逻辑检查及原生模组扫描通过。
105组中英文语言键和占位符、39张贴图透明与占格、种袋和酒瓶比例检查通过。
新酒瓶已目视检查浅蓝色、透明背景和四格导出；其余38张运行贴图与0.1.9哈希相同。
本次没有接管游戏，也没有修改存档内容。

A Fellow Townsman Arrives v1.0
Internal version: 1.0.0

Finishing changes
1. Renamed Hydroponics & Brewing to A Fellow Townsman Arrives (老乡来了).
2. Ancient Fruit Wine changes from dark purple to light blue. Its dark teal neck, fruit label and slender silhouette remain; the bottle still occupies 1 by 4 cells.
3. Retains the 22-cell ingredient inventory, 1/4/9-cell crop sizes, giant guarantee and finished-wine liquid-transfer protection.

Updating and compatibility
The pelican_cultivation ID and Mods/PelicanCultivation folder are retained. Replace the existing installation; do not install a duplicate.
Restart the game before loading a save. Existing Ancient Fruit Wine uses the new sprite; scores, aged values, genetics, growth and guarantee records are retained.
The runtime contains only required loading files. Documentation, workshop copy, source, original artwork and prompts are kept outside it.
Bilingual workshop descriptions are provided. Nothing was uploaded or published to Steam during this update.

Verification
Compilation, 13,064 logic checks and the native mod scanner passed.
105 bilingual keys/placeholders, 39 transparent sprites, native footprints and seed/bottle export proportions passed.
The new bottle was visually checked for its light-blue palette, transparency and four-cell export. All other 38 runtime sprite hashes match 0.1.9.
The game was not controlled and save contents were not edited during this update.
