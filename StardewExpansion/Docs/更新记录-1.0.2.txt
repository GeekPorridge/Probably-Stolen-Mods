老乡来了 v1.0.2 — 每周三、周六来访

老乡改为每周三、周六各来访一次，独立于退休农夫及其解锁状态。首周对应经营第3、6天，此后每7天循环。
每次种子库存和售价保持原样，每周有两次进货机会。第三次实际到访的赠种保底继续沿用，不按周数重算。
读档及访客队列刷新不会重复安排同一天的到访；已完成交易后同一天不会再次安排。旧版尚未到场的非日程访客按新日程修正，正在进行的交易和本次库存保留。
中英文初次及回访对话、说明和工坊介绍同步更新。
本版只调整来访日程；养殖扩展仍在构思阶段，小牛、小猪和新增鱼种尚未加入。

A Fellow Townsman Arrives v1.0.2 — Wednesday and Saturday visits

The Fellow Townsman now visits once every Wednesday and once every Saturday, independently of the retired farmer and his unlock state. Operating days 3 and 6 start the schedule, repeating every seven days.
Per-visit seed stock and prices stay unchanged, giving two weekly restocking opportunities. The seed gift still counts actual visits and retains its existing records.
Loading or regenerating the client queue cannot duplicate a same-day visit. Completed visits are not requeued. Legacy off-day queued visits follow the new schedule; an ongoing trade and its stock are retained.
English and Simplified Chinese dialogue, instructions and Workshop descriptions have been updated.
This update changes the visit schedule. Husbandry remains in design; calves, piglets and added fish species are not included yet.
