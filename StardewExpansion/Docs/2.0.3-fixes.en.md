# Stardew Expansion 2.0.3 / Night Walker 1.1.1

- Custom livestock cuts now inherit their source animal's ownership. Loaded product trays rebind acceptance to the live owning cage.
- Custom brews resolve their actual drink names instead of retaining the native Bloomberry wine name. Player custom names remain preferred when requested.
- Movable fertile chicken eggs retain inherited genes and cage-only incubation.
- Automatic green serum, when enabled, checks every at-risk owned animal before nightly disease checks. Low immunity qualifies even without existing disease. Real doses are consumed; active effects are not stacked. Native serum effects and disease rules still apply.
- Legacy non-meat feed without provenance migrates once without adding calories. Feeding and forecasts share its classification. Mixed incompatible diets receive an explicit diet warning rather than a misleading zero-night estimate.
- Intro-skipping runs initialize husbandry before the first sleep instead of waiting for initial-item or morning-report hooks.
- Four reservoir fish have body-mass-based harvest caps. Fully fed adult reference limits: midnight carp 600, largemouth bass 900, tuna 1600, lava eel 1200 kcal. Existing fish and carcasses also use these limits. Vanilla fish and legendary trophies are unchanged.
- Night Walker 1.1.1 restores the omitted custom icon key before native icon resolution.

The third-party Better Item Tooltips mod supplies the Shift-to-view-description behavior. Keep ShowDescription enabled and disable CollapseDescription in its settings. These Workshop packages do not overwrite another mod's preferences.

Built and checked against the locally installed game assemblies using isolated native-method regressions and the game's mod scanner. Unity rendering and scene boundaries are fixtures; no player save was opened or modified. A complete in-game acceptance pass remains outstanding. Previously lost or sold items are not recreated.

The 2.0.2 hydroponic harvest-mode fix is retained. Station Scavenging 1.5.6 and Metal Wine Bottles 0.2.1 do not require updates. Workshop publication remains manual.
