Stardew Expansion 2.0 r7 — Current machine effects and complete recipes

Durations below assume default 8 work/night. Prices are base values before native quality, market demand and negotiation. The three named seafood dishes have fixed base prices independent of their calories. The manifest version remains 2.0. This reference defines current rules; r6 and earlier files remain historical.

Purchase and resale
The Friday Townsman Artisan sells all four machines and the Artisan Handbook. Wednesday retains seeds and Planting/Husbandry books; Saturday retains husbandry stock and bait.
Retail / empty-chassis resale ceiling: Cheese Press 450/72; Oil Press 600/96; Barrel 450/72; Grill 500/80.
Negative demand can lower the offer. Positive chassis modifiers cannot exceed the ceiling. Player-supplied batteries, modules, wine and other contents retain independent value.

Barrel
Purpose: automatically improve whole finished native wine and added fruit wines through native maximum and then Silver, Gold and Iridium. No power, water, modules or bottle consumption. The wine variety remains unchanged. A single 10x4 storage bay holds up to ten whole individual bottles, each with its own saved progress. No recipe selector or progress panel.
Reject beer, pale ale, chili spirit, counterfeit wine, bottles without their full drink charges, empty bottles and fermenting wine. A stacked item cannot bypass the ten-bottle limit. Tier-0 finished wine is eligible if it meets the same whole/non-counterfeit conditions.
Starting at native tier 0–3: reach Reserve (tier 4) after 6 nights and native maximum (tier 5) after 14 nights.
Starting at Reserve: reach native maximum after 8 nights.
Starting at native maximum: begin star aging immediately.
After native maximum: 7 nights -> Silver +25%; another 10 nights -> Gold +35%; another 15 nights -> Iridium +50%.
Total nights from barrel entry, for a newly started bottle:
- Native tier 0–3: native maximum 14; Silver 21; Gold 31; Iridium 46.
- Reserve: native maximum 8; Silver 15; Gold 25; Iridium 40.
- Already native maximum: Silver 7; Gold 17; Iridium 32.
Star value is a final multiplier after native quality: x1.25, x1.35 or x1.50. A new star replaces the old barrel modifier; the stars do not stack with each other or with r4's +10% Barrel Character. Existing bottle progress is retained, and the old modifier is replaced upon reaching a star. Native aging data remain intact. Removal pauses progress; re-entry and changing barrels cannot duplicate a night's progress or compound rewards.

Cheese Press
Battery and universal modules; 8x4 input and 8x4 output. Process 1–4 matching eligible ingredients per batch; a full batch is not required.
1 egg, at least 150 kcal -> 1 mayonnaise, 180 kcal, base value 30; 1 night.
1 milk, at least 350 kcal -> 1 cheese, 350 kcal, 2x2 cells, value 70; 2 nights.
1 goat milk, at least 400 kcal -> 1 goat cheese, 400 kcal, 2x2 cells, value 100; 2 nights.
Each consumes one ingredient item. Ingredients below the recipe's nutrition threshold are ineligible. Auto order: egg, milk, goat milk.

Oil Press
Battery and universal modules; 10x4 input and 8x4 output. Older 8x4 inputs expand on load while preserving contents.
1 black truffle, at least 300 kcal OR 2 small truffles, each at least 125 kcal -> 1 truffle oil; 2 nights. Up to two oil bottles/batch; large and paired small truffles can be combined. Oil is 200 kcal, 2x2 cells, base value 150.
1 whole chili spirit + 4 poppies + 4 empty wine bottles -> 4 poppy perfumes + 1 returned empty wine bottle; 2 nights.
1 whole chili spirit + 4 fairy roses + 4 empty wine bottles -> 4 fairy rose perfumes + 1 returned empty wine bottle; 4 nights.
Use wine bottles with no water or other contents; generic chemical flasks do not qualify. Flowers cannot be mixed. The consumed spirit bottle is returned empty, including spirit made with the finished-drink cheat ID.
Perfumes occupy 2x2 cells and have three native charges. Poppy: value 100, 20% two-way influence. Fairy Rose: value 160, 30%. Both are contraband level 2. Effects apply to the current customer visit and take the strongest native/mod scent; no charge is used without a customer or under an equal/stronger effect. Flower quality changes sale value, not influence strength.
Auto order: complete Poppy perfume, complete Fairy Rose perfume, truffle oil.

Grill — general food
Battery and universal modules; 8x4 input and 8x4 output. Every current Grill recipe takes one base night.
General meat recipes accept raw beef, pork, chicken, goat and recognized finfish meat. Lobster and sea cucumber have dedicated recipes below and cannot become generic grilled fish. Rat meat, legendary collectibles and already cooked food are ineligible.
Plain: 1–1800 kcal of one raw meat kind -> the same total calories in 1–2 cooked portions. Raw calories beyond the batch limit remain in the input.
Per 900 raw kcal, base cooked values are chicken 120, goat 185, beef 165 and pork 190. Fish uses consumed raw fish value x1.25.
Chili: 1800 kcal of one raw meat kind + 2 chili peppers, each at least 120 kcal -> 2 spicy portions; +30 base value per portion.
Truffle: 1800 kcal of one raw meat kind + 1 truffle oil, at least 200 kcal -> 2 truffle portions; +85 per portion.
Mayonnaise fish: 1800 kcal of fish meat + 1 mayonnaise, at least 180 kcal -> 2 portions; +25 per portion.
Seasoning calories enter the finished food and are divided between the two portions. Seasoned recipes require 1800 kcal of a single meat kind, without combining different animal types.

Grill — seafood dishes
1 raw lobster-meat portion -> 1 Baked Lobster; Food and Luxury; fixed base value 350.
1 harvested sea-cucumber portion, or 1 whole sea cucumber -> 1 Scallion Sea Cucumber; Food; fixed base value 200.
1 whole Tuna + 1 whole Lava Eel + 1 whole Midnight Carp + 1 whole Largemouth Bass -> 1 Sashimi Platter; Food and Luxury; fixed base value 600.
All three take one base night and occupy 3x2 cells. Lobster and sea-cucumber batches handle up to four eligible source portions of their respective recipe. One source item makes one dish. Sashimi requires one of each named whole fish and makes one platter per batch; eggs, fry and generic fish-meat cuts cannot substitute.
Dish nutrition is the actual ingredient calories x1.2. For a whole sea cucumber, use its current harvestable meat calories; for Sashimi Platter, use the sum of the four fish's current harvestable meat calories before applying x1.2. For example, 900 kcal of lobster meat makes a 1080-kcal Baked Lobster with base value 350. Another eligible lobster-meat size changes nutrition while retaining that base value.
These three base prices are fixed and do not automatically scale with calories. Native ingredient/module quality still applies; pellet-tainted whole fish retain the resulting trash-grade penalty. Existing r5 dishes receive a one-time 20% nutrition upgrade and the fixed base value, without repeated upgrades on later loads.
Finished dishes cannot be fed back through the Grill. Lobster and sea cucumber remain excluded from generic grilled-fish production.

Grill — bait
1 wheat, at least 300 kcal + 50 ml clean water -> 6 ordinary bait.
6 ordinary bait + 1 mayonnaise, at least 180 kcal -> 6 premium bait.
Both take one base night. Up to four groups/batch, or 24 bait. Both occupy 2x2 cells and each stack holds at most 20.
Clean water means at least 97% water; heavy metals, chemical contaminants, microbes, organic waste, acid and base must each be at most 0.1%. Only the required water is removed, leaving its vessel and any remaining water.
Full automatic priority: complete Sashimi Platter -> Baked Lobster -> Scallion Sea Cucumber -> premium bait -> ordinary bait -> chili meat -> truffle meat -> mayonnaise fish -> plain meat. An active batch retains its chosen recipe; unused ingredients remain for later batches.
Old one-cell bait stacks expand only when their current container has a valid 2x2 position. A full container retains every unit, stack identity and metadata at the old footprint, shows a space-needed hint and retries automatically. Migration neither merges stacks nor moves bait to a different container.

Shared powered-machine rules
Default processing is 8 work/night with base energy use 8 per working night. Universal modules change performance, efficiency or quality; speed affects duration without changing ingredient quantities. Ingredient quality and quality modules affect product value.
Batteries and modules have dedicated bays and are rejected by raw input. Only owned machines and ingredients are used. Input is locked during an active batch. No power pauses work. If the whole output batch cannot fit, ingredients remain intact while waiting, without repeatedly charging the blocked final step. Resources are consumed only after the full batch can be placed. Recipe selectors, progress bars and extra control bays are absent.

Chicken eggs and incubation
Healthy adult hens produce one ordinary 150-kcal egg/night at an additional 150-kcal food cost. With a healthy adult male of the same species in the cage, a hen can also produce an additional 150-kcal fertile egg at a separate 150-kcal cost. Chicken production is automatic, with no laying/breeding selector or management panel.
The fertile-egg base chance is 50%, adjusted by both parents' fertility and health; at least three nights must pass between successful fertile eggs. Both parents' genes are preserved. Sterilized chickens cannot breed, but hens retain ordinary egg production. Ordinary eggs use the product inventory and laying a new fertile egg requires cage space, independently. A blocked destination waits without charging its corresponding food cost.
A fertile egg hatches after two effective incubation nights and remains able to hatch after its parents leave the cage. Its one-cell chick replaces the existing one-cell egg in place; even a cage with every other cell occupied needs no additional empty cell for that replacement. Only a replacement that genuinely cannot fit waits while preserving the egg. Fertile eggs are protected from processing, feeding and automatic slaughter.

Bait and reservoir use
Aquaculture I reveals the reservoir immediately after Dr. Jackson and before the Upper District. The card stays visible without bait and explains the unavailable departure. At least one ordinary or premium bait must be inside the selected travel backpack before leaving. Owned inner bags count; ground and side-pocket bait do not. The departure boundary checks the selected backpack before native transfer and the actual equipped backpack when already outside.
Saturday Townsman stock is five ordinary bait; Aquaculture II adds three premium bait. The reservoir has no purchases or free bait. The first entry each night records actual backpack bait as the allowance, without a fixed eight-cast cap; side pockets and ground items do not count, and re-entry does not reset it.
Select ordinary or premium bait using its own button. Casting immediately spends one actual selected backpack bait and one allowance. That payment is final on success, failure or abandonment; it is never charged a second time for the same cast. A full ground inventory keeps the catch pending and blocks new casts until collection. Pending catches and bonus eggs can be collected without another bait or allowance. Legendary cooldown begins when its catch is successfully placed.
Hold or release left mouse or Space to steer the cursor after a moving green target. Night fishing difficulty ranges from 1 (easy) to 5 (hard), default 3, and changes the next cast. A fight keeps the difficulty it started with. At the default, ordinary targets move at 0.42 with a 16%-wide zone; legends move at 0.75 with 10–13%-wide zones. These default target speeds are 50% above r6. Cursor response scales with difficulty so tracking control is retained across all five levels. The native-style bottom-right panel adapts to the visible viewport and stays within the screen.
Esc, settings and return confirmations pause fight input, progress and timers. Closing or cancelling those menus resumes without counting paused time. The top X is hidden; use the native bottom Return. Unexpected panel hiding or destruction at the reservoir restores the current fight automatically without resetting the allowance or charging another cast.

Legendary Fish Activity
Each unlocked reservoir night has a 20% chance of a native Legendary Fish Activity event that lasts one night. Its transit event card includes the title, description and effect; an Artisan Handbook page explains the rules. The nightly decision is saved on both success and failure. Reopening menus, revisiting or reloading cannot reroll it, including the night the reservoir is first unlocked or the update is installed.
Ordinary bait has base legendary chances of Legend 0.5%, Crimsonfish 1%, Glacierfish 1% and Mutant Carp 1%. During activity these become 1%, 2%, 2% and 2%. Premium bait independently multiplies each by 1.5, giving activity-night chances of 1.5%, 3%, 3% and 3%.
Legend retains a 12-night cooldown; Crimsonfish, Glacierfish and Mutant Carp retain independent 8-night cooldowns. A legend in cooldown returns only its own interval to ordinary fish, without increasing another legend's chance. Activity does not change junk chances: 8% for ordinary bait and 3% for premium. Cooldowns still start when the caught legend is successfully placed on the ground.
Midnight Carp, Largemouth Bass, Tuna and Lava Eel, including their eggs and fry, have bilingual descriptions and flavor text. New creation, older saves and native aging updates preserve those narratives. Their diets, culture timing and breeding rules are unchanged.
