水培与酿酒扩展 0.1.5

1. 小麦啤酒、淡啤酒改为横2格、纵3格，共6格；保留原图宽高比和矮宽瓶身。
2. 增加自动补种，默认开启，可在模组设置中关闭。覆盖本机登记的7种原版植物和8种新增植物。
3. 补种来源依次为本机输出区、主背包、仓库，只取1粒同种现有种子。普通容器可查找，其他机器内部的种子不取用。
4. 保留种子基因与品系数据；堆叠种子拆分后互不影响。多年生保留母株，缺种时等待。生长、耗水和耗电沿用原版周期。
5. 增加8种种子的中英文完整初始词条报告，效果值直接读取原版公式。Ⅱ级不按简单翻倍计算。
6. 旧啤酒扩为6格时检查容量；无空间时保留原位置和价值，腾出后再展开。
7. 保留0.1.4已确认正常的作物鼠标命中修复与老乡向下偏移。

重新启动游戏后加载原存档生效。开关位于本模组设置的“水培 / Hydroponics”分组。
安装时只替换模组文件，不修改存档。旧版已备份；当前仍为本地测试版，未发布Steam工坊。
已通过编译、4,720项逻辑回归、原生模组扫描、79组中英文文本及34张资源检查。
六格啤酒和自动补种尚待本轮实机验证。

Hydroponics & Brewing 0.1.5

Wheat Beer and Pale Ale now occupy six cells (2 by 3) with their original broad silhouettes.
Automatic replanting defaults to on and is configurable. All seven native and eight added plant species are supported.
One matching seed is taken from the machine output, main inventory, then storage. Ordinary containers are searched; seeds inside other machines are excluded.
Actual strain data, edited traits and heat are retained. Split seeds have independent state. Perennials retain their parent; missing seeds cause waiting. Native growth/power/water cycles remain.
Added bilingual initial trait reports generated from native formulas. Rank II effects use the native curve.
Finished beer expansion checks capacity and safely waits if all areas are full, preserving the item and its aged value.
Includes the user-confirmed 0.1.4 hit-surface and downward portrait fixes.

Restart before loading your save. The switch is in this mod's Hydroponics settings section.
Installation replaces mod files only, with a backup of the previous version. Not published to Steam Workshop.
Compilation, 4,720 regression checks, native scanning, 79 bilingual keys and 34 sprites passed. In-game testing of the new 0.1.5 features remains pending.
