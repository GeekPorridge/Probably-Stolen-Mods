Stardew Expansion 2.0 r7 — Local acceptance build
Author: Xiao Zhou; Probably Stolen 0.46D; Simplified Chinese / English

This revision retains the mod ID pelican_cultivation and the manifest version 2.0. Earlier revision notes remain historical references. Current rules are described here and in All-machine-recipes-2.0-r7.en.txt. No Workshop or Nexus publication is part of this update.

Getting started
Restart the game to load the new assembly and art together. Buy seeds and planting/husbandry books on Wednesday, machines and the Artisan Handbook from the Friday Townsman Artisan, and husbandry stock on Saturday, subject to native ability unlocks.
Saturday stock includes five ordinary bait. Aquaculture II adds three premium bait while retaining the five ordinary bait. Bait can also be made in the Grill. Both bait types occupy 2x2 cells and stack to 20. Carry it inside the actual travel backpack before entering the reservoir; ground items and side pockets do not count.
Aquaculture I opens the Space Station Recycling Reservoir on the evening map, immediately after Dr. Jackson and before the Upper District. Its card remains visible without bait and explains why departure is unavailable. Carry at least one ordinary or premium bait inside the selected travel backpack before departure; owned bags nested inside it count. Ground items and side pockets do not. The departure check uses the selected backpack before native transfer, then the equipped afterhour backpack when already outside. There is no reservoir bait shop or first-visit bait gift.

Handbooks and item presentation
Planting, Husbandry and Artisan handbooks use independent illustrated windows with the vanilla hydroponic guide assets, 24-point measured body text, fixed-scale dragging and independent page state. Genetics chapters appear after gene editing unlocks. The paper now blocks machine hover information behind it, preserving a clear reading area.
Ancient, Strawberry, Starfruit and Blueberry wines share matching 1x4 bottle silhouettes and display heights. Liquid colors are Ancient #0F7281, Strawberry #DB171F, Starfruit #E4C51F and Blueberry #4C5DB6. Wine appendices are hidden while ordinary native quality, alcohol, aging and status remain available.

Crops and brewing
Twelve crops: Parsnip, Potato, Corn, Wheat, Hops, Strawberry, Starfruit, Ancient Fruit, Chili Pepper, Blueberry, Poppy and Fairy Rose. All twelve have distinct mature hydroponic overlays aligned to the native machine canvas.
At the default 8 work/night with normal water and power: Chili takes 4 nights initially, regrows in 2, and yields 4; Blueberry takes 7, regrows in 3, and yields 6; Poppy takes 4 and yields 4 flowers; Fairy Rose takes 8 and yields 4. Poppy and Fairy Rose require replanting. Genes, care and modules can change actual results.
Chili uses 1x2 cells; wheat and flowers use 2x2. Blueberries have small/normal/giant variants occupying 1/4/9 cells. Flowers are for perfume, display and sale, not eating or feeding.
Two chili plus 750 ml water in a wine bottle make chili spirit: 3 nights with ordinary yeast, 6 naturally, 2 with super yeast. Base value is 100. Two blueberries plus 750 ml water make blueberry wine: 4 nights with ordinary yeast, 8 naturally, 2 with super yeast; base value 150. Native quality and integer valuation still apply.
Regular Ancient Fruit harvest returns full fruit without a seed and retains the perennial parent. Switch to breeding before a later harvest for seed production. Scavenging and the Townsman's seed gift remain available.

Chickens, goats and feeding
Chickens mature at 4 nights and become elderly at 18. Maintenance is 60 kcal/night; healthy adult hens lay one 150-kcal egg/night at an additional 150-kcal cost. Egg base value is 18.
Goats mature at 8 nights and become elderly at 28. Maintenance is 80 kcal/night; healthy adult females give one 400-kcal milk every 3 nights at an additional 400-kcal cost, without requiring a prior birth. Milk base value is 60. Gestation is 6 nights, default litter one, and postpartum recovery 3 nights.
Cattle, chickens and goats eat plant food only; pigs remain omnivores. Feed dispensers track newly ground plant calories. Unidentifiable legacy mixed feed is retained, but fresh crops must be ground to establish eligible herbivore reserves.
Chicken breeding is automatic when healthy adult males and females share a cage. The ordinary 150-kcal egg continues as its own output. An eligible pair can also produce an additional 150-kcal fertile egg at an extra 150-kcal cost, with a 50% base chance modified by both parents' fertility and health and at least 3 nights between successful fertile eggs. Sterilized chickens cannot produce fertile eggs; hens can still lay ordinary eggs. A fertile egg retains both parents' genes and hatches one chick after 2 effective incubation nights, even if the parents are later moved or removed.
Ordinary eggs use the product inventory; laying a new fertile egg requires space inside the cage. The two outputs are independent: a full destination retains the corresponding production opportunity without charging its food cost. An existing fertile egg is replaced by a one-cell chick in its own cell, so a full cage needs no additional empty cell for that replacement. Only a replacement that genuinely cannot fit waits while preserving the egg. Fertile eggs are protected from processing, feeding and automatic slaughter. Elderly, hungry or ill stock pause relevant production without accumulating retroactive batches.

Artisan production
The Friday Artisan sells Cheese Press 450, Oil Press 600, Barrel 450 and Grill 500. Empty-chassis resale ceilings are 72, 96, 72 and 80 respectively. Positive demand, events and perfume cannot lift owned chassis above these ceilings; negative demand can lower offers. Contents retain independent value.
Cheese Press: up to four complete matching ingredients. Egg -> mayonnaise, 1 night, value 30; cow milk -> cheese, 2 nights, 70; goat milk -> goat cheese, 2 nights, 100.
Oil Press: one standard truffle or two small ones -> one truffle oil, 2 nights, up to two bottles/batch, value 150. Its 10x4 input fits a complete perfume recipe.
Perfume: one whole chili spirit + four matching flowers + four empty wine bottles -> four perfume bottles, returning the spirit bottle empty. Poppy takes 2 nights, value 100; Fairy Rose takes 4, value 160. Each has three uses and a 20%/30% two-way effect on the current customer's prices. Only the strongest native/mod scent applies; no charge is wasted without a customer or under equal/stronger influence. Both are contraband level 2. Flower quality changes sale value, not bargaining strength.
Grill: plain and seasoned beef, pork, chicken, goat and recognized finfish meat; ordinary and premium bait; and three seafood dishes. Lobster meat makes Baked Lobster (Food, Luxury), sea cucumber makes Scallion Sea Cucumber (Food), and one whole Tuna + one Lava Eel + one Midnight Carp + one Largemouth Bass makes Sashimi Platter (Food, Luxury). All three take 1 night and occupy 3x2 cells. Their fixed base values are 350, 200 and 600 respectively; each dish contains its actual ingredient calories multiplied by 1.2. Calories do not automatically rescale these prices. Existing r5 dishes receive a one-time 20% nutrition upgrade and the fixed base value. Lobster/sea cucumber do not become generic grilled fish. See the complete recipe reference for quantities, priorities and batch limits.
The three powered machines detect complete recipes automatically and process overnight. Native universal modules affect speed, efficiency and quality. Working input is locked; no power pauses work, and full output retains ingredients until the whole batch can fit.

Barrel quality
The unpowered Barrel holds up to ten whole individual bottles in a 10x4 bay. It accepts native finished wine and added fruit wines, excluding beer, pale ale, chili spirit, counterfeit, fermenting, empty or partly drunk bottles. There is no recipe selector or progress panel.
Production quality or lower reaches native Reserve after 6 barrel nights and native maximum after 14. A bottle starting at Reserve takes 8 nights to reach native maximum. A bottle already at native maximum begins the star stages directly.
From native maximum: 7 more nights -> Silver, +25% value; 10 additional nights -> Gold, +35%; 15 additional nights -> Iridium, +50%. These are cumulative 7/17/32 nights after native maximum. Each star replaces the preceding barrel modifier and multiplies value after native quality; the three bonuses do not stack.
Existing bottle progress and native aging data are retained. The old r4 +10% Barrel Character is replaced when a star is reached, without stacking with the new star. Taking a bottle out pauses its barrel progress; reinserting or changing barrels cannot duplicate nights or rewards.

Reservoir fishing
The compact fishing panel uses the native scavenging menu style at the bottom right and adapts to the visible viewport. Choose ordinary or premium bait with its dedicated selection button, then cast. Hold or release left mouse or Space to steer the cursor and follow the moving green target zone. Keeping the cursor inside that zone advances the catch. Legendary fish move faster and use a narrower target zone.
Night fishing difficulty ranges from 1 (easy) to 5 (hard), with 3 as the default. A changed setting applies to the next cast and does not alter a fight already in progress. At the default, target speeds are 0.42 for ordinary fish and 0.75 for legends, 50% faster than r6; green-zone widths are 16% of the track for ordinary fish and 10–13% for legends. Cursor response scales with difficulty to preserve tracking control across all five levels.
The first reservoir entry each night records the total ordinary plus premium bait inside the actual travel backpack as that night's allowance. There is no fixed eight-cast cap. Reopening or re-entering does not refresh the allowance. Ground items and side pockets do not contribute. Casting requires an actual backpack unit of the selected bait and immediately consumes that unit plus one allowance.
Success, failure and abandonment do not refund that payment or charge it again. A catch that cannot fit on the ground remains pending; free space and collect it before casting again. Collecting a pending catch or bonus eggs never spends a second bait or allowance.
Opening Esc, settings or a return confirmation pauses the current fight and its timer. Closing or cancelling those menus resumes the fight without counting the paused time. The top X is hidden; use the native Return button at the bottom. If the fishing panel is unexpectedly hidden or destroyed while the player remains at the reservoir, it restores the current fight automatically without refreshing the allowance or charging another cast.
Ordinary catches have a 20% chance of matching unfertilized eggs. Midnight Carp are omnivores; Largemouth Bass, Tuna and Lava Eel are carnivores. Their initial sources are reservoir catches/bonus eggs; the Townsman does not sell these fish or eggs. All four fish, their eggs and fry have bilingual descriptions and flavor text; those descriptions remain correct for new items, old saves and later growth stages.
Ages below are hatch / individual juvenile / adult / elderly / mortality risk, starting at fertilization and effective culture:
Midnight Carp 2/4/8/24/36; four fry, 12% nightly female egg chance, extra 80 kcal.
Largemouth Bass 2/5/10/28/40; three fry, 10%, 100 kcal.
Tuna 3/6/14/36/50; two fry, 8%, 160 kcal.
Lava Eel 3/6/16/40/54; two fry, 6%, 160 kcal.
Natural breeding requires healthy adult males and females of the same species, with native male fertilization. Elderly fish stop breeding; full tanks retain eggs/fry and pause new stock. Meat yield follows cumulative feeding. Lava Eel provide eel meat; the others provide generic fish meat.
Legendary fish are collectibles/sale goods, not farmable food: Legend 1600 / 12-night cooldown; Crimsonfish 900, Glacierfish 1000 and Mutant Carp 1100 / independent 8-night cooldowns. Cooldown begins when the caught legendary is successfully placed on the ground.
Ordinary bait: legendary chances 0.5%, 1%, 1%, 1%; junk 8%. Premium multiplies each legendary chance by 1.5 and lowers junk to 3%. A legendary in cooldown returns its interval to ordinary fish without changing other legendary chances. Fishing does not spend scavenging attempts.
Each unlocked reservoir night has a 20% chance of the native Legendary Fish Activity event, lasting one night. Its transit card shows the event title, description and effect, and the Artisan Handbook includes an event page. During activity, all four legendary probability intervals double; premium bait independently keeps its x1.5 multiplier. Cooldowns and junk chances remain unchanged. The day's roll is saved whether it succeeds or fails, so reopening, revisiting or reloading cannot reroll that night.

Installation and acceptance
Update only the currently enabled instance of this mod ID and restart the game. Where local/Workshop duplicates exist, the game log identifies the actual loaded folder. Existing configuration and save identifiers are retained. Runtime contains only DLL, manifest, locale CSVs and PNG assets; source, original art and documentation stay in the source package.
Old one-cell bait stacks resize safely when a valid 2x2 space is available in their current container. If it is full, the old footprint and every bait unit remain intact, with a space-needed hint and automatic retry. Resizing does not merge stacks or move them to another container.
The F8 cheat menu supports the registered machine, handbook, finished-drink and seafood item IDs. Native abilities still apply. The complete IDs and recipes are in All-machine-recipes-2.0-r7.en.txt.
