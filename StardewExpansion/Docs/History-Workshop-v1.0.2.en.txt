[h1]A Fellow Townsman Arrives[/h1]
A fellow townsman reaches the station with a few packets of seeds. Grow familiar crops, care for your hydroponics, and sell the harvest at your counter or turn it into a bottle worth waiting for.

[h2]Eight crops and a familiar visitor[/h2]
Adds parsnip, potato, corn, wheat, hops, strawberry, starfruit and ancient fruit.
The Fellow Townsman visits once every Wednesday and once every Saturday, selling seeds for seven crops independently of the retired farmer's unlock. Each visit has fixed stock; clear counter space during his visit for the remaining offers. Advanced seeds unlock with plant gene editing. Ancient seeds come from exploration and a one-time seed gift.

[h2]Care for your crops and grow a giant[/h2]
Potato, corn, strawberry, starfruit and ancient fruit have Small, Regular and Giant sizes occupying 1, 4 and 9 cells. Wheat, hops and parsnip stay Regular, two cells.
Harvest care of at least 95 and effective quality bonus of at least 50% unlock a 10% chance per batch for at most one giant. After ten qualified misses on the same machine and species, the next qualified harvest guarantees one. Giants replace a crop in the batch without increasing total yield.
Automatic replanting uses matching seeds from existing supplies and retains their actual genetics and edits. Ancient fruit offers normal and breeding harvest modes.

[h2]Five brews using native winemaking[/h2]
Wheat Beer, Pale Ale, Strawberry Wine, Starfruit Wine and light-blue Ancient Fruit Wine.
Use two crops of the same species per bottle, with optional native yeast. The ingredient inventory expands to 22 cells, fitting two giant crops and yeast.
Ingredient condition, giant bonus points, water and yeast determine quality. Size does not add a separate wine-price multiplier. Wheat Beer and Pale Ale gain Chilled (+20% value) in a powered fridge at its daily cycle and cannot enter the wine ager. The three fruit wines retain native aging value gains. Finished added wines cannot be poured into water-storage containers.

[h2]Installation and compatibility[/h2]
Includes English and Simplified Chinese. Uses native hydroponics, plant gene editing, showcase sales and aging. Station Scavenging and Metal Wine Bottles are optional companions.
Formerly Hydroponics & Brewing. Version 1.0.2 keeps the original mod and save identifiers. Restart after updating and continue your existing save. Saved beer retains historical aging value but will not age further.
For manual installation, place Mods/PelicanCultivation in the game's Mods directory. Do not install a duplicate copy.
