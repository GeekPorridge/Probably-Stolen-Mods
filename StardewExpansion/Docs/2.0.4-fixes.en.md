- Fixed empty husbandry output: native machinery panels wrap inventories in `MachineryIntake`. Resolve the actual grid through native storage traversal, and stop appending duplicate trays on loading/morning initialization.
- Repair legacy cage storage indices before native child-item restoration, preserving dispenser/butcher placement, product identities and animal state. Historical missing products are not recreated. Blocked chicken/goat output is now reported.
- Guard invalid live-cattle/pig/chicken/goat weights. Restore the last valid weight, or the native stage minimum for an old zero record. Preserve positive weight loss, health, disease, hunger and death state; do not invent carcass yield. The player's save did not reproduce the reported illness-to-zero transition; explicit invalid-weight recovery is covered by regressions.
- Chili spirit uses a 2×3 footprint. Legacy items resize after inventories finish loading. A full inventory retains the old footprint and contents until a safe retry; the pending-space tooltip now appears for spirit as well as beer.
- Chili spirit no longer appreciates through overnight, rack or direct native aging callbacks, and no longer advertises aging. Historical value and age remain intact. Reusing its bottle for fruit wine restores fruit-wine aging.
- Harden gameplay readiness using the existing player store and actual inventory graph. Handbook, sleep and transit entry points check initialization; loading and transit restore reservoir registration when needed. Access was verified with the available save; not all legacy-save states are covered.
- Food eggs use 1×2 cells and mayonnaise uses 2×2. Art is re-exported from the original atlas with preserved proportions and transparency. Legacy resizing preserves item identities and quantities and never merges stacks during migration.

Fully exit and restart the game after updating. Enable one copy of `pelican_cultivation`. Historical missing night logs are not fabricated from current livestock; new reports start with the next normal overnight settlement. The cheat menu's date-skipping command does not process nights.

Ice cream is non-plant food. Pigs can use it, while cattle, goats and this mod's chickens require plant feed. Add wheat, corn, Nutrifruit or another accepted plant food and blend it manually or enable automatic blending. Known non-plant reserves are not relabelled. Age, health, hunger and production intervals still apply.

Automatic serum and feces handling retain existing player settings. Equipment information depends on the installed equipment and nightly conditions. Night Walker remains at 1.1.1.

See the delivery's live-game validation record and automated verification log for evidence and boundaries. The original player save was backed up and restored after testing.
